The short answer

A good bathroom remodel contractor will show you a license you can look up, have their insurance agent send you a certificate, pull the permit in their own name, give you an itemized written bid that names the waterproofing method, and ask for a small deposit with the rest paid as work is finished. If any one of those is missing, keep looking.

What pushes your price up or down

Raises cost
  • Moving the toilet, drain, or shower valve
  • Water damage or rot found after demo (about 1 in 3 renovators in the This Old House survey said the job cost more than expected)
  • Curbless or walk-in showers that need the floor recessed
  • Custom glass and large-format tile
Keeps cost down
  • Keeping fixtures where the plumbing already is
  • Choosing materials before the bid, not after
  • Prefab wall panels instead of tile in the shower
  • Booking outside the spring rush

Red flags that end the conversation

  • Cash only or a big "materials deposit" before anything is scheduled.
  • "We don't need a permit for this" on a job that moves plumbing or wiring.
  • Today-only pricing that expires when the salesperson leaves.
  • No street address or a business name that does not match the license.
  • A one-line bid: "Remodel bathroom, $14,000."

Remodeling for an older adult or a wheelchair user?

A general remodeler can do beautiful work and still miss the details that make a bathroom safe to use with limited mobility: a shower entry low enough to roll or step over, a seat, blocking in the walls for grab bars, a hand-held shower, slip-resistant flooring, and enough clear floor space to turn around.

For that kind of job, ask each bidder how many accessible bathrooms they have built and to show you one. Questions people ask before hiring

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in 2026?

This Old House's survey of 1,000 recent renovators put the average at $15,586, with most projects between $6,456 and $24,715. Fixr gives a $6,000 to $18,000 range. Small cosmetic updates cost far less; moving plumbing or fixing hidden water damage costs more.

How many quotes should I get for a bathroom remodel?

Three written, itemized quotes is the usual rule. Fewer gives you nothing to compare; more than four mostly costs you evenings.

How much deposit should a bathroom contractor ask for?

Keep it small. California caps home-improvement down payments at $1,000 or 10% of the contract, whichever is less. Other states vary, but a large upfront payment before any work is a warning sign everywhere.

Does a bathroom remodel need a permit?

Usually yes when plumbing, electrical, ventilation, or walls change. Swapping a vanity or repainting often does not. Your local building department decides, and the contractor should pull the permit in their own name.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

A cosmetic refresh can take a few days. A full gut remodel typically takes several weeks once work starts, plus time for permits, inspections, and special-order materials.
